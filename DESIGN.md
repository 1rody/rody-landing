# RODY.CITY DESIGN

Minimalism design isnt just about hide details or cut edges, minimalism is about why. so here in design.md i will explain why and the DEFINITIONS of patterns on every part of my landing and why the code style and colors are made like it

## DUAL STYLE (ON LANDING)
the dual style, two colors for EASY visualization and color pallet simplification.

the dual color style like SHADCNUI or NEXTJS basic project was chosen to Hilight the information and at the same time, show to the user an diverse spectrum of colors.
White on light colors represent all colors unified and dark represent the space for construction, this dual theme was chosen to use the BLACK infinite space and THE white construction for EVERYTHING on light.

and why lightmode?
Because everyoone had your own preference, on lightmode the concept inverts itself like the coulor pallete, AN BLANK space FOR BUILDING ideas, the black structural bone for the ideas and your systems.
